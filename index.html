From requirements to interactive prototype

Problem: POS, inventory, recipe and store operations require connected, consistent workflows rather than isolated screens.

My role: Independently define functional requirements, user flows, business rules and UI prototypes, then iterate through hands-on verification.

01 · Analyze

Define company/store boundaries, product pricing, ordering rules and inventory dependencies.

02 · Prototype

Build responsive React/Vite screens for order taking, SKU configuration, recipes and suppliers.

03 · Validate

Review scenarios such as variant recipe fallback, pay-later edits and role-based navigation.

Status: Ongoing prototype. Backend authentication, persistent data and production deployment are separate future development work.
